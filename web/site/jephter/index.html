Jephter Studio

A private publishing tool that writes, narrates and uploads short videos to one YouTube channel that its owner has authorised.

What this application does

  • An AI model drafts a short script for one video.
  • A text-to-speech voice — a persona chosen by the channel owner — narrates it.
  • The narration is assembled into a vertical short video.
  • The finished video is uploaded to the single YouTube channel Jephter (@TODO-jephter-handle) through the YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

Only the owner of Jephter. There is no sign-up, no public account, and no other channel can be connected. The Google account that authorises the app is the account that owns the channel.
